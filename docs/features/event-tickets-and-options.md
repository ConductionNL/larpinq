# Event tickets and options

A LARP weekend is not one price. Players pay more than crew, NPCs often pay nothing, early sign-ups pay less, and catering is a choice with its own price. Larpinq lets a game master set that up per event, and lets a player choose on their registration. Larpinq records what was chosen at the listed price; the amount to pay and the payment are handled by shillinq.

## Set up tickets for an event

1. Open **Ticket types** and add one per kind of participant: a name, a role (player, crew, NPC or other) and the listed price in cents. A price of 0 is a free ticket.
2. For an early bird price, give a ticket type an **On sale until** moment and add a second ticket type at the normal price. After that moment only the second one is offered.
3. Give a ticket type **Places** when only so many people can have it, such as 20 crew places. A sign-up beyond that goes on the waiting list, even when the event itself has room.
4. Switch on **Only with a code** for a ticket type you hand out yourself, and make an **Access code** that unlocks it, with an optional valid window and number of uses.
5. Open **Registration options** to add extras such as **Full catering, vegan**. An option with **Places** is refused once it is full.

The event page lists its ticket types, options and codes.

## Choose tickets as a player

On your registration, open the **Tickets** tab. Pick a ticket type and any options, and type a code if you have one and press **Use code** to see the tickets it unlocks. Press **Save choices**.

Larpinq checks every choice when you save: the ticket type must be on sale, a hidden ticket type needs a valid code, and a full option is refused with a message. The price shown is the listed price at the moment you choose. When a game master changes a price later, your choice keeps its price.

## See what people chose

Game masters open the **Choices** tab on the event. It counts the accepted registrations per ticket type and per option, so the kitchen knows how many vegan meals to cook. It shows counts only, never a money total.
