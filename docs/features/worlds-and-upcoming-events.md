# Worlds and upcoming events

Keep your campaigns apart and see what is coming up next. An event belongs to a world, you narrow the lists to one world at a time, and the lists open on upcoming events.

## The world of an event

A game master sets an event's world on the event form, in the **World** field. The event page shows it, and the Events list has a **World** column.

## Work in one world

Most lists (Characters, Abilities, Skills, Items, Conditions, Effects and Events) and the dashboard have a **World** switcher at the top.

1. Open the switcher and pick a world.
2. The lists and the dashboard counts show only that world's characters, rules and events. The page says "Only Aldmoor is shown."
3. Choose **Show all worlds**, or pick **All worlds** in the switcher, to see everything again.

Larpinq remembers your choice, also on your next visit and on another device. The choice is yours alone: other players and game masters keep their own.

Things to know:

- An event, character or rule without a world is shown under **All worlds** only, not beside a single world's own.
- A page you open directly, for example from a link, always opens, whatever world is active.
- When the world you chose is archived or deleted, larpinq goes back to **All worlds**.

## Upcoming events first

The Events list opens on **Upcoming**: events that start today or later, soonest first. Choose **Past** to see earlier events.

The dashboard shows the next six upcoming events. **View all events** opens the Events list.
