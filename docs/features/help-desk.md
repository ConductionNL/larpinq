# Help desk

Players have questions: can I bring my own bow, is there a vegan meal, my character's skill is missing. The help desk keeps those questions in Larpinq instead of in someone's private mail. A player asks, the game masters answer from one list, and the player hears back and can reply until the question is closed.

## What the player does

1. Open **Help** in the menu and add a question. Give it a subject and the question itself, and if it is about an event or a character, pick that too.
2. The question shows on the Help page as **open**. Only you and the game masters can read it.
3. When a game master answers, you get a Nextcloud notification and the question shows as **answered**. Open it to read the answer under **Messages**.
4. Not quite what you meant? Add a message to the question. It is open again and back on the game masters' list.

Once a game master closes a question, nobody can add a message to it any more. Ask a new question instead.

### In the portal

A player who uses the portal has a page **Vragen aan de spelleiding**. There they choose **Ask a question**, see their questions with their state, and read the answers. The portal tells them when the state of one of their questions changes, for instance when it is answered.

## What the game master does

1. The **Open questions** count on the Dashboard shows how many questions are waiting. Choose it, or open **Help desk** in the menu.
2. The Help desk shows the questions waiting for an answer, the oldest activity first. A question that has had no message for more than three days is marked **Waiting too long**. The tabs show the answered and the closed questions.
3. Open a question, assign it to yourself in **Assigned to** if you like, and add your answer under **Messages**. The question becomes answered and the player is notified.
4. When nothing more needs saying, choose **Close**.

Every game master is notified when a new question comes in.

## Good to know

- A question belongs to one player. Other players never see it.
- Attachments are not possible yet. Ask the player to share a file another way, for instance through Nextcloud Files.
- Answered questions are not turned into a public list of frequently asked questions.
